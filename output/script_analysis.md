# Limitless Script Report

## About the Script
**Title**: Limitless  
**Author**: Leslie Dixon  
**Genre**: Science Fiction/Thriller  
**Date**: 2011

**Plot Summary**:  
The film's narrative follows Eddie Morgan, an aspiring writer who gains access to a powerful nootropic drug known as MDT that dramatically enhances his cognition and productivity. Experiencing the world with newfound clarity and processing information at lightning speed, Eddie embarks on a journey that swiftly propels him to success in both his personal and professional life.

However, the allure of the drug comes with a steep price. As his dependency grows, Eddie suffers alarming side effects, rendering him vulnerable to hallucinations and perilous memory lapses. The stakes escalate when adversaries – each driven by their motives – emerge to exploit the secrets Eddie now harbors.

The script melds elements of mystery, suspense, and the thrill of discovery with a rich exploration of ambition and the ethical considerations surrounding the use of intelligence-enhancing technologies. Through Eddie's turbulent rise and fall, the film poses a poignant question about human potential: What would we sacrifice to become 'limitless'?

Ultimately, "Limitless" teases the exhilarating – and perilous – possibilities of expanding the human mind, inviting viewers to consider their boundaries and the moral complexities of shattering them.

## Locations
- **New York City**: The bustling, vibrant atmosphere of New York City plays a significant role in the film. As the primary setting, it represents ambition and opportunity, with iconic locations such as Wall Street portraying the financial hub of the world where Eddie seeks his fortune.
- **Eddie's Apartment**: Starting as a cluttered, dimly-lit space reflecting his initial state of chaos and stagnation, the apartment transforms as Eddie becomes more successful. The setting mirrors his mental and personal growth.
- **Times Square**: A prominent New York landmark, Times Square is where Eddie experiences a vivid and surreal moment, showcasing the sensory overload he feels on MDT.
- **Carl Van Loon's Office**: A lavish corporate office representing power and influence. This is where Eddie's ambition encounters high stakes as he negotiates major deals and faces powerful adversaries.
- **The Seaside Cabin**: A remote location where Eddie seeks refuge. It symbolizes his escape from the chaos of the city and a place of reflection, albeit temporarily, from his dizzying rise.
- **The Nightclub**: A high-energy, fast-paced environment where Eddie's heightened intellect and charisma are on full display. The nightclub scene contrasts with the more cerebral moments of the film.

## Characters
- **Eddie Morgan**: The protagonist of the story. Initially a struggling writer in his 30s, Eddie's life takes a significant turn after discovering MDT, a powerful drug that enhances his mental capabilities. As he ascends to the heights of success, he must navigate the challenges of addiction, ethical dilemmas, and the constant threat of those who wish to control or harm him.

  **Suggested Actors**: Aaron Paul, Joel Edgerton, Sebastian Stan.
  
- **Lindy**: A professional woman with a complicated emotional connection to Eddie. Although supportive, her relationship with him becomes strained due to the dangers associated with MDT and the lifestyle it brings.

## Props
- **MDT-48 pills**: These mysterious pills are central to the plot. They are the experimental drug that significantly enhances Eddie Morra's cognitive capabilities, allowing him to access 100% of his brain.
- **Eddie's laptop**: A crucial tool for Eddie, especially in the early scenes, enabling him to write efficiently and showcase his boosted mental abilities.
- **Eddie's wardrobe transformation**: As Eddie's success grows, his sense of style changes dramatically, reflecting his newfound confidence and status.
- **Carl Van Loon's office**: A symbol of power and influence, representing the high-stakes world Eddie is stepping into.
- **Floor-to-ceiling windows**: Often used in scenes to depict Eddie's expansive view of the world and his place in it after taking MDT-48.
- **Financial stock charts/documents**: Used to depict Eddie's rapid rise in the financial world, showcasing his ability to understand and capitalize on complex financial data.
- **Brook Atwood's bugged pen**: A sly tool used by Atwood for recording crucial conversations. Represents the surveillance and constant threat surrounding Eddie.
- **Physical books and manuscripts**: Eddie's script and the books he reads at lightning speed as he absorbs vast amounts of knowledge while under the influence of the drug.
- **Wall Street buildings**: Symbolic of the fast-paced and cutthroat financial world Eddie enters, embodying both opportunity and danger.
- **Hospital visit scene**: Where Eddie learns about the dangerous side effects of MDT-48, underscoring the theme of the perilous consequence of ambition.
- **NYPD officers and arrest scene**: Illustrates the legal boundaries Eddie brushes against as he spirals due to overuse of the drug.
