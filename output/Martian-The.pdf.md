# The Martian Script Report

## About the Script
**Title:** The Martian  
**Author:** Drew Goddard  
**Genre:** Science Fiction, Drama  
**Date:** 2015  

**Plot Summary:**  
The narrative follows astronaut Mark Watney, who becomes stranded on Mars after a botched mission causes his crew to assume he is dead and leave the red planet. Watney must rely on his ingenuity, determination, and extensive scientific knowledge to try to survive until rescue is possible. The story unfolds through his solitary video logs and interactions with NASA and his crew, showcasing his resourcefulness, such as growing potatoes using Martian soil and reclaimed water. Simultaneously, back on Earth, NASA rallies to mount a daring rescue, exemplifying international collaboration and the human spirit's resilience.

## Locations
- **Mars Surface:**  
  The barren and desolate terrain where Mark Watney finds himself stranded. Features rocky landscapes, dust storms, and vast plains where Watney grows potatoes and undertakes various survival tasks.
  
- **Ares III Hab:**  
  The habitation module designed for the Ares III crew on Mars, acting as Mark Watney's shelter and primary workspace where he strategizes his survival and attempts communication with Earth.
  
- **Pathfinder:**  
  The landing site of the 1997 Mars Pathfinder mission, which Watney discovers and uses as a communication device to establish contact with NASA.
  
- **Hermes spacecraft:**  
  The spacecraft used by Watney's crew to travel to and from Mars. It is depicted both in transit between Earth and Mars and as a site of the daring rescue operation.
  
- **NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL):**  
  Located on Earth, JPL is the nerve center for much of NASA's Mars missions, where scientists and engineers work tirelessly to figure out solutions to aid in Watney's rescue.
  
- **NASA's Johnson Space Center:**  
  Where NASA's key personnel, including Director Teddy Sanders and Flight Director Mitch Henderson, coordinate the overall mission's strategic planning and decisions.
  
- **China National Space Administration (CNSA):**  
  Reflects international cooperation and support, as they aid NASA by providing a booster rocket necessary for the rescue mission.

## Props
- **ARM Computer:** Utilized for data logging and communication, it is central to Mark Watney's interaction with NASA and his fellow astronauts.
- **Specimen Drill:** Used for collecting soil samples, essential for Mark's attempt to cultivate crops on Mars.
- **Water Reclaimer and Oxygenator:** Vital life-support systems that recycle water and produce oxygen from the Martian atmosphere, crucial for sustaining life in the habitat.
- **Potatoes:** Represent food production, grown using Martian soil and water derived through chemical reactions with hydrazine.
- **Hydrazine Tank:** Sourced from the Mars Descent Vehicle, it provides the chemicals necessary for Mark's water production efforts.
- **Hab Canvas:** Used for habitat repairs, highlighting Mark's improvisational skills and adaptability in dire situations.
- **Communications Antenna:** Essential for contact with NASA but is damaged early in the story, adding a layer of tension as Mark strives to fix it for communication.
- **Space Suit:** Necessary for survival outside the habitat, enabling Mark to explore and work on the Martian surface.
- **Data Stick:** Contains old TV episodes, offering psychological relief and comfort during Mark's isolation on Mars.
