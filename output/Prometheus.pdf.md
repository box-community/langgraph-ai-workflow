# Prometheus: A Movie Script Report

## About the Script

**Title:** Prometheus  
**Author:** Jon Spaihts  
**Genre:** Science Fiction  
**Date:** Created before October 2023  

**Plot Summary:**  
The movie script for "Prometheus" is a science fiction adventure that follows a team of explorers as they journey to a distant moon, guided by ancient star maps, in search of humanity's origins. On this moon, they encounter the remnants of an advanced alien race known as the Engineers and uncover dark secrets about creation and destruction. Throughout their mission, they awaken deadly creatures, leading to a perilous struggle for survival.

The storyline delves into themes of existential questioning, the quest for knowledge, and the potentially dire consequences of human ambition. Significant character arcs include Professor Martin Holloway, driven by an unwavering pursuit of truth; Dr. Jocelyn Watts, exemplifying scientific dedication; and David the Android, embodying ambiguity, with motives that align with corporate interests but remain suspect. The narrative unfolds against the backdrop of corporate desires clashing with the ethical and survival challenges faced by the crew.

## Locations

- **Earth (12,000 B.C.):**  
  The movie opens on Earth during prehistoric times. A pale-skinned Engineer is seen standing at the edge of a massive waterfall. The setting is lush, with untouched nature surrounding the scene, portraying a pristine and primordial Earth. The Engineer has a pivotal encounter which is reluctant and sacrificial in nature.

- **Earth (2089):**  
  Thousands of years later, in 2089, the narrative shifts to an archaeological site on the Isle of Skye, Scotland. The landscape is rocky and rough, with ancient caves where Dr. Jocelyn Watts and Professor Martin Holloway make an astonishing discovery that propels the story forward.

- **Starship Prometheus:**  
  The crew embarks on their journey aboard the starship Prometheus, which is well-equipped and technologically advanced, signifying the immense effort and resources invested in the mission. This location serves as a temporary home and hub for the characters, complete with living spaces and scientific facilities.

- **LV-426:**  
  The main setting of the movie, LV-426 is a desolate and barren moon in the Zeta2 Reticuli system. Defined by its harsh, rocky landscape, extreme weather conditions, and an atmosphere of impending danger. This moon houses the ancient pyramid, where much of the plot unfolds.

- **Ancient Pyramid:**  
  Located on LV-426, the pyramid is colossal and foreboding, laden with dark corridors, cryptic symbols, and cavernous chambers. It is the dwelling of the Engineers' ancient technology and lethal creatures, surrounded by an eerie and dangerous aura.

- **Room of Heads:**  
  Within the pyramid, the Room of Heads is a pivotal location marked by large, sculpted heads and bizarre artifacts. This room contains life-sustaining panels and hints at the Engineers' advanced bioengineering skills, playing a crucial role in the unfolding mystery.

- **Engineer Ship:**  
  Another key location is the derelict Engineer spacecraft, which is buried beneath the pyramid. It represents a treasure trove of advanced technologies and answers, as well as the source of existential threats to humanity if its secrets are unleashed.

## Characters

- **Dr. Jocelyn Watts:**  
  A key archaeologist whose drive for discovery mirrors humanity's eternal quest for knowledge. She's the beating heart of the mission, fueled by the promise of unraveling humanity's mysteries.

  Watts's intellectual counterpart and partner in discovery. His enthusiasm and willingness to embrace the unknown highlight the adventurous spirit central to the mission.

  The wealthy backer whose interest in power and legacy spurs much of the film’s events. Weyland’s character reveals themes of human ambition, mortality, and the lengths one will go to achieve greatness.

  These ancient beings are the enigmatic key to the film's exploration theme, representing a forgotten history and confronting humanity with the question of its origins and potential destruction.
  - **Suggested Actors:**

- **The Crew:**  
  A melting pot of human attributes: bravery, curiosity, skepticism, and fear. Their collective journey underscores themes of survival, human nature, and the collective versus individual goals.

## Props

- **Excavator and Obelisk Prints:** Central to the plot, facilitating discovery of alien artifacts and providing clues about the Engineers and humanity’s origins.

- **Holographic Imager and Seed Probe:** Advanced technology aiding scientific investigation, revealing biological mysteries and understanding the alien environment.

- **Facehugger:** Iconic prop symbolizing the horror and danger posed by the alien life cycle, embodying existential threat.

- **Power Saw and Combat Rifle:** Emphasize physical challenges and dangers faced by the crew, underscoring survival themes.

- **Cryogenic Freezer:** Highlights long-duration space travel and preservation of human life, emphasizing the futuristic setting.

- **Engineer Head:** A tangible connection to the ancient civilization, key to understanding their biology and technology, deepening the narrative mystery.

## Author

### Accomplishments

- **Breakthrough with 'Prometheus':**  
  Spaihts gained significant recognition for co-writing "Prometheus" with Damon Lindelof. This film, directed by Ridley Scott, is part of the expanded universe of the "Alien" franchise.

- **Expansion into Blockbusters:**  
  He has been part of writing teams for major Hollywood blockbusters, displaying a knack for crafting engaging science fiction narratives.

### Other Movie Scripts

- **Doctor Strange (2016):**  
  Spaihts co-wrote the screenplay for Marvel's "Doctor Strange," introducing audiences to the realm of mystical arts within the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

- **Passengers (2016):**  
  Wrote the screenplay for "Passengers," a space-based romantic science fiction film starring Jennifer Lawrence and Chris Pratt.

- **The Mummy (2017):**  
  Worked on the screenplay for this attempted reboot of the Universal Pictures' monster series, starring Tom Cruise.
